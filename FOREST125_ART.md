# Forest & Gunner — artwork record

All five sheets were created with the built-in image-generation tool. Existing Broxton artwork was used only as a visual-style reference; Forest is a new character. Later Forest sheets used the first Forest sheet as their design reference. Transparent alpha is preserved. Runtime import isolates each figure and uses a fixed scale for its sheet, with source foot/body anchors recorded in `dist/forest125-data.js`.

## Final prompt set

- `dist/assets/sprites/forest-motion125.png`: 4×4 full-body hunter atlas. Mature outdoorsman, 45–48, rugged hazel eyes, predominantly dark beard graying at sides and chin, olive curved-brim cap, olive rolled-sleeve field shirt, utility belt, khaki cargo trousers and hiking boots. Strong functional build with a slightly thicker waist; no bodybuilder exaggeration. Knife guard, walking, running, tucked jump, descending kick, three distinct knife slashes, downward stab, knife stow/crossbow draw, firing, cycling and crossbow stow. Compact crossbow strapped to back except when drawn; one knife and one crossbow. Transparent background, no text, panels or shadows. Detailed illustrated 16-bit-inspired arcade proportions matching the game's style.
- `dist/assets/sprites/gunner125.png`: 4×3 isolated brown/black German Shepherd–Malinois working dog, dark facial markings, alert ears and olive harness without armor. Consistent anatomy and scale. Idle, opposite walking strides, sitting, extended/gathered running, leap, landing, bark, lunge, sustained bite, rest. Natural four-legged gait, no stretching, gore, people, text, panels or shadows; transparent background.
- `dist/assets/sprites/forest-grapple125.png`: Same Forest identity and outfit in 16 isolated poses. Front hold, knee, front/back spinning-knife views, crouched waistlock, planted leg drive, backward arch and deep grounded bridge, supported spinebuster lift and drive, rise, kneedrop, rear upper-body restraint, back-to-back hold, bending hip flip and crouched grounded knife follow-up. No enemy included. Maintain dimensions and equipment; knife sheathed during empty-hand holds, drawn only for knife poses; no jump in the arch poses.
- `dist/assets/sprites/forest-extra125.png`: Same Forest in eight full-body poses: focused knife guard, dog command, forearm block, stagger, fully flat grounded body, kneeling getup, low sidestep and back-facing slash. Same costume, cap and equipment; transparent separated cells, no labels or shadows. Do not scale crouched or lying poses to standing height.
- `dist/assets/sprites/forest-heel125.png`: Two full-body Forest poses: airborne knee-raised preparation and an upright-torso heel drop with the striking leg extending steeply downward/forward, heel below hips and toes flexed upward. Rear leg bent. Explicitly exclude a horizontal flying side kick. Keep head, limb, boot and equipment proportions equal between drawings; transparent background, no enemies, text, panels or shadows.

The original generated sheets are also retained in the generation history. All runtime assets above are committed with the game source.
