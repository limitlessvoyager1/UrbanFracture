ROOT CAUSE AND CORRECTION
The old shared renderer required an undeclared Canvas module and used a legacy
allowlist that stopped short of current fighter owners. Its image error handling
could also allow dependent processing to fail misleadingly after missing assets.

The replacement uses an explicit reviewed manifest in production index order:
179 modules,73 actual draw-owner attachments,1,041 decoded image requests.
It includes twelve omitted late fighter modules and the hospital material helper.
Mixed scene/fighter owners are retained; general page/UI controllers are excluded.
Missing/changed/unreviewed dependencies cannot silently produce a green result.
Nested image work and final readiness are drained; missing assets stop the load
before cascading decode work. Rejected readiness chains remain recorded errors.

Development-only package/lockfile: @napi-rs/canvas1.1.1,Playwright1.57.0.
Verified platform: Windows x64,Node22.21.0,Chromium143.0.7499.4/revision1200.
