ROOT CAUSE
Reina's active contact override and diagnostic candidate used Combat134.box in ground space while rendering raised Saito during martialFly. A strike below his raised body could intersect that old rectangle and apply7HP. Weapon and martial contact paths already handled elevation, so changing the generic base rectangle would risk subtracting height twice.

CORRECTION
Add one Plane304.reinaHurtbox helper:retain existing base bounds and subtract the existing renderer world elevation from top/bottom once. Both actual Reina contact and its diagnostic candidate call that helper. Zero elevation returns the original unchanged base rectangle. Keep Combat134.box, height calculation, segment geometry, active frames, move data, lane/range/protection rules and Saito's defenses unchanged.
Valid contacts retain their existing damage and timings. Eligibility intentionally changes for previously phantom below-body contacts; this is not a promise that the old erroneous damage or its resulting interrupted flight trajectory stays identical. Grounded gameplay must and does retain exact parity. Niko phase labels and all other melee-family geometry remain separate.
