# URBAN FRACTURE — SPECIAL AGENT NIGHTSHADE
## Complete Playable Character Implementation — Revised Master Specification
### Combat, Animations, Firearms, Ammunition Economy, Enemy AI, Controls, and QA
### Normal Difficulty Baseline | 60 FPS

---

# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Create Special Agent Nightshade as a fully playable hero in Urban Fracture.

Nightshade is an agile, adaptable field operative capable of fighting effectively at close, medium, and long range.

Her archetype is:

**JACK OF ALL TRADES — MASTER OF NONE**

She uses:

- A six-round handgun.
- A collapsible baton.
- A wrist-mounted laser and electrical device.
- A poison-dart pen.
- Ground taser gadgets.
- Compact explosives.
- Agile kicks.
- Grappling techniques.
- Tactical counters.

Her gun is powerful but limited by ammunition, firing recovery, positional accuracy, and enemy defensive intelligence.

Her melee combat must remain effective when she has no ammunition.

## Mandatory development requirements

1. Preserve the existing combat engine.
2. Create Nightshade's complete moveset.
3. Implement R2 as the reusable Unique Action button.
4. Implement six-round handgun ammunition.
5. Add six upright bullet icons beneath her defense meter.
6. Implement guaranteed scripted ammunition pickups throughout existing and future levels.
7. Balance scripted ammunition according to encounter difficulty, expected playtime, and level length.
8. Implement uncommon randomized ammunition drops.
9. Implement enemy defensive behavior against bullets.
10. Preserve universal grab and counter rules unless explicitly overridden.
11. Implement proper synchronized grappling animations.
12. Prevent sprite scaling, gliding, floating, and animation locks.
13. Test Nightshade with full ammunition, partial ammunition, and zero ammunition.
14. Follow the mandatory incremental Work implementation workflow.
15. Produce a verified QA report after every implementation gate.

**Do not attempt to implement the entire character in one unverified Work job.**

Create a recoverable checkpoint before modifying the project.

# PHASE 7 — SCRIPTED AMMUNITION SYSTEM
## CRITICAL BALANCING REQUIREMENT

When Nightshade is the active playable hero, a limited number of guaranteed ammunition pickups must appear at designated locations throughout each level.

These are scripted pickups.

They must always appear at their designated locations when Nightshade plays the level, unless already collected during that run.

They are separate from random enemy and destructible-object drops.

### Important

Do not determine the scripted ammunition budget solely from the physical length of the level.

Use:

1. Expected gameplay duration.
2. Number of mandatory encounters.
3. Number of enemies.
4. Enemy difficulty and composition.
5. Number of locked encounters.
6. Boss presence.
7. Available melee opportunities.
8. Expected ammunition consumption.
9. Random ammunition drop opportunities.
10. Difficulty setting.

### Design principle

A short level with several difficult mandatory encounters may need more ammunition than a long level containing mostly exploration.

However, additional difficulty must not automatically result in excessive ammunition.

---

## PHASE 8 — SCRIPTED AMMUNITION BUDGET

Use the following initial guidelines for Normal difficulty.

| Level type | Expected duration | Small clips | Large clips |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Short | Under 10 minutes | 1 | 0 |
| Medium | 10–20 minutes | 1–2 | 0 |
| Long | 20–35 minutes | 2–3 | 0 |
| Very Long | Over 35 minutes | 3–4 | 0–1 |

These values are initial level-wide guidelines, not per-section allowances.

### Encounter-based adjustments

Work should evaluate:

- Number of high-threat encounters.
- Number of enemies resistant to stagger.
- Frequency of enemies capable of dodging gunfire.
- Availability of cover.
- Number of mandatory boss encounters.
- Expected player opportunity to fight without bullets.

### Adjustment limits

If a level has unusually difficult encounters, allow a small adjustment within the approved ammunition budget.

Do not automatically double scripted ammunition because a level has many enemies.

### Large clips

Six-bullet clips must remain rare.

A guaranteed large clip should generally be reserved for exceptional long levels or a specifically justified encounter.

### Critical requirement

**The scripted ammunition budget must be calculated for the entire level before pickups are placed.**

Do not independently award the full budget to every section.

# PHASE 9 — SCRIPTED PICKUP DISTRIBUTION

Distribute guaranteed ammunition across the level rather than clustering it.

### Placement rules

- Place pickups at meaningful progression points.
- Avoid placing all clips at the beginning.
- Do not place clips inside inaccessible scenery.
- Do not place them on enemy spawn points.
- Do not place them beyond locked arena boundaries while the player is trapped.
- Do not place them in locations that require impossible movement.
- Do not create guaranteed ammo immediately before every enemy encounter.

### Recommended distribution

For a level with three small scripted clips:

- One during the early-to-middle portion.
- One during the middle portion.
- One during the later portion.

These are placement guidelines, not fixed percentage requirements.

### Boss considerations

A difficult boss may justify one nearby small clip, but this must be included in the level's total ammunition budget.

Do not add an unlimited or automatically respawning ammunition supply.

# PHASE 11 — RANDOM AMMUNITION DROPS

In addition to scripted pickups, ammunition may occasionally drop from enemies and destructible objects.

### Initial Normal difficulty probabilities

| Source | 3-bullet clip | 6-bullet clip |
|---|---:|---:|
| Regular enemy | 8% | 1% |
| Strong enemy | 12% | 3% |
| Eligible destructible object | 10% | 2% |

These probabilities apply to eligible loot events.

### Requirements

- Integrate with the existing loot Director.
- Preserve health and other pickup categories.
- Do not duplicate loot events.
- Do not create multiple ammunition rewards from one event unless explicitly scripted.
- Prevent repeat farming.

### Gunfire kill adjustment

Enemies defeated primarily through gunfire have their ammunition-drop probabilities reduced by 50%.

### Shortage protection

The loot Director may modestly increase small-clip eligibility after a prolonged ammunition shortage.

However:

- The adjustment must be bounded.
- It must not create guaranteed ammunition.
- It must not refill the gun after every fight.
- It must not override scripted ammunition limits.

### Difficulty

Easy may provide slightly more ammunition.

Hard may provide slightly less.

Do not remove essential scripted pickups solely because Nightshade is performing well.

# PHASE 12 — AMMUNITION ECONOMY VALIDATION

### Test A — Firearm-heavy player

- Ammunition is eventually depleted.
- The player cannot sustain unlimited gunfire.
- Random drops do not continuously refill the magazine.
- Scripted pickups do not trivialize encounters.

### Test B — Balanced player

Player alternates melee, grabs, specials, and gunfire.

Verify:

- Ammunition remains useful.
- The player can conserve bullets.
- Gunfire provides meaningful advantages.
- Melee combat remains important.

### Test C — Melee-focused player

Player rarely uses bullets.

Verify:

- Pickups do not accumulate beyond six bullets.
- Ammo does not interfere with other pickups.
- The player is not penalized for conserving ammunition.

### Test D — Zero-ammunition player

Player has no bullets for an extended encounter.

Verify:

- All non-ammunition-dependent moves function.
- Standard combos remain effective.
- Grabs remain useful.
- Dodge and counters remain useful.
- The player can defeat ordinary enemies without ammunition.
- No mandatory encounter requires gunfire unless explicitly designed and separately approved.

### Critical requirement

**Nightshade must remain a capable playable character with zero bullets.**

Her gun should be an advantage, not a requirement for basic survival.

# PART IV — ENEMY DEFENSE AGAINST BULLETS

- Sidesteps.
- Dodges.
- Ducking.
- Repositioning.
- Moving out of the firing lane.
- Solid environmental cover.
- Specialized ballistic protection where explicitly defined.

Ordinary melee blocking does not stop bullets.

### Armor

Bullets ignore armor damage reduction.

Armor may still provide stagger resistance.

### Important

Do not give enemies universal bullet immunity.

## PHASE 14 — DEFENSIVE AI

Enemies respond to visible aiming cues rather than directly reading R2 input.

### Initial conditional defense-attempt rates

| Enemy type | Rate |
|---|---:|
| Basic thugs | 10–15% |
| Workers | 5–10% |
| Weapon specialists | 15–20% |
| Heavy enemies | 10–15% |
| Martial artists | 25–35% |
| Spectre | 30–40% |
| Bosses | 15–35%, character-specific |

These are attempt rates, not guaranteed successful dodges.

### Requirements

- Enemy must perceive Nightshade.
- Enemy must be actionable.
- Enemy must have time to react.
- Enemy must have the appropriate defensive move.
- Enemy cannot cancel mandatory recovery.
- Enemy cannot evade after a confirmed hit.
- Enemy must obey cooldowns.

### Adaptation

If Nightshade repeatedly fires from the same lane:

- Skilled enemies may reposition.
- Agile enemies may increase evasive movement.
- Some enemies may seek cover.
- Aggressive enemies may close distance during firing recovery.

Do not allow perfect bullet prediction.

## PHASE 15 — STAGGER LIMITS

Prevent firearm stun-locking.

### Requirements

- First eligible shot may cause brief stagger.
- Additional shots during the same stagger window deal damage without repeatedly restarting it.
- Strong enemies resist stagger more effectively.
- Bosses generally resist repeated ordinary bullet stagger.
- Ordinary bullets do not normally cause knockdown.
- No permanent stagger loops.

# FINAL DESIGN GOAL

Special Agent Nightshade must be an agile, versatile fighter who can handle enemies at every range without dominating every discipline.

Her handgun should feel powerful, but ammunition must remain valuable.

Her baton, kicks, grabs, counters, poison, electrical devices, and explosives must remain important parts of her fighting style.

Scripted ammunition should provide a modest, predictable supply across each level, adjusted for encounter difficulty and expected playtime rather than simply physical level length.

Random drops should supplement that supply without creating unlimited gunfire.

**Nightshade must be capable and enjoyable with zero ammunition, but strategically stronger when bullets are available.**
