ROOT CAUSE
Playback's actor snapshot defaulted to actionable when a living target had flight but no action/down flag. The nested Continuity296 snapshot correctly reported fall, producing contradictory diagnostic labels. Raw hitReaction298 fields mix contact history with a later observation of stun/shock clearance; without explicit labels, actualSeconds0 after Niko launch could be mistaken for completed recovery.

CORRECTION / FIELD CONTRACT
Playback now labels living flight as fall. Existing defeated, held and authored action phase policy is retained; this is not a broad state-machine rewrite.
New read-only HitReactions298.diagnostics supplies:
-contactOutcome/contactAt:last recorded contact outcome/time, ornull when absent.
-currentPhysicalState:the existing Continuity296 projection for this recorded frame.
-commonOffensiveReady:the existing common-readiness projection, not universal per-move permission.
-stunLockActive:the existing stun/shock guard; not a complete recovery guard.
-stunClearObservedAt:alias of raw releasedAt, preservingnull and0.
-stunClearElapsedSeconds:alias of raw actualSeconds, preservingnull and0.
The helper is exposed in playback's reactionDiagnostics and audit actor diagnostics. Existing raw hitReaction, outcome, locked and actualSeconds fields remain compatible. No raw receipt, timestamp or event is changed, reset or delayed. In particular, Niko launch still clears stun in the contact tick:clean contact, fall state, stun-clear elapsed0 and offensive readinessfalse are simultaneously valid.

The production diagnostic panel now distinguishes 'last contact' in timeline titles and accessible labels, uses 'no action' rather than idle for absent actions, and displays a visible note:contact outcome is history; physical state describes the recorded frame; stun-clear time is not flight or total recovery. Detailed exported notes retain the raw-field compatibility explanation. Scrubbing remains observational and does not update old records with live state.
