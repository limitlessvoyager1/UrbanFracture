# Niko: defensive specialist

Checkpoint: `checkpoint-before-niko185` at `aac63101b2f32bb69e22195cf474ebd74f4f5c12`. Existing appearance, levels and other heroes' moves are preserved. Niko has110HP; Reina now has105HP.

Niko uses four separately confirmed palms/kick hits (3/3/4/6), and three separately pressed front-grab hits (4/4/6). Ordinary damage budgets are deliberately below existing heroes: throat14, armbar12, rear knee/kick14, reverse DDT15, ground submission7, grab special18, Thunder Palms18, Blur Dash11, aerial6/7, mounted aerial8, two-sided headbutt10each, dash counter10. Recovery prevents damage-throughput compensation. Thunder Palms charges for3 seconds and releases through the existing shared owner. Dodge retains the universal2-second cooldown.

Arm Injury halves punches, throws and applicable weapons for12 seconds. Leg Injury halves kick damage and visible locomotion for10seconds. Concussion produces intermittent0.25second interruptible stuns over8seconds. Chance/duration resistance: standard1/1, strong.65/.75, mini.45/.6, boss.3/.4. Essential scripted boss mechanics are protected, but bosses are not universally injury-immune. Statuses neither stack nor refresh; a newly applied injury can grant a capped25% move-base bonus once. Damage context distinguishes kicks from arm-dependent weapons and excludes environmental hazards. Independent seeded status RNG does not consume weather RNG.

Blocking uses half the existing chip factor, with ordinary defense consumption. A successful block arms one12% offensive-action bonus for1.2seconds. Dodges and counter preparation cannot waste or stack it. Dedicated manual counter animations remain intentionally reserved: timed deflection, eligibility, defense costs and a registration hook exist; no final finishing choreography is invented. Forward Dash Counter uses the existing generic deflect reaction and deals10damage, below future manual-counter targets.

All pair sequences use the established GrappleRecovery119/Forest125 sessions, ground locks, deferred lethal settlement, fixed combat depth and cleanup. Committed portions are protected, startup/recovery vulnerable. Ground submissions have a7second per-victim cooldown, reject protected/owned targets and require depth alignment. Only Niko can initiate dedicated grounded attacks; existing other-hero scripted throw follow-ups remain enabled. Clear-out requires two eligible opponents and spends nothing when unavailable.

Artwork: two new alpha WebP atlases, one constant import scale per atlas, sole anchors and a face crop for the portrait. Exact generation prompts and source references are in `design/niko185-image-prompts.json`. Injury overlays, limp cadence and concussion stars preserve actor identity. No existing sprite atlas was replaced.
