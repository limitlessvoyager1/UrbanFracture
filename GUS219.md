## Fairness and AI

115HP, speed88. Ordinary eligible armed weights40/25/25/10; unavailable options removed. An observed heavy miss may add a standalone overhead punish option; repeated observed blocks increase throw weight. Jump observation is separate from ordinary decision cadence so the20frame aerial reach can occur during the actual jump. The existing shared anti-air reaction delay and probability still apply. Being engaged with another enemy increases decision/positioning priority by25%, not speed or damage. No guards, feints, perfect prediction, indefinite chains or recovery cancels.

Three-point/two-commitment Director148 remains unchanged. Weapon attacks reserve2points, grabs1.5, stomp2. Exact contacts and active tails are registered; the capture takes over the reach reservation. Sticky/electrical/synchronized restraints retain existing admission protection. Offscreen or out-of-arena Gus cannot start attacks/heal; after recovery he reenters the visible bounds with HP, cooldowns, weapon loss, injuries and healing decision on the same instance.

Frontal120-degree damage-taking armor exists only for late committed crowbar windows (last3startup frames, or last8 before overhead, through active contact). Rear/side, early startup, reach grabs and recovery interrupt normally. HP damage still passes. The4frame metallic-white feedback is scoped to genuine resisted stagger. Force/grab/armor-break attacks, Niko injuries/concussion and Orion's resisted electrical stun keep their established rules. Strong wake protection becomes damage-taking for up to1second, followed by a fair recovery gate. Recovery deadlines survive interruption.

## Healing and disarm

Exactly one random roll on the first transition below25%HP: `<.25` grants one future actionable attempt. Retrieval consumes at66frames, restoring28.75HP (25% of115), capped at maxHP; total84frames. No restoration during startup. A valid interrupt before consumption marks the attempt spent and drops one native29HP pickup outside scenery, collectible through normal hero/Gunner item behavior. Native unrelated death loot remains separate. No retry after drop, no pickup duplication. Permanent disarm latches weapon loss, cancels weapon attack/hitbox and uses unarmed punch/kick plus physical slam/stomp/throw; no regeneration.

## Compatibility boundary

There is no global Adaptive Encounter or Locked Battle controller in the current source. `Gus219.metadata` supplies ordinary strong/weapon-frontline role, complexity3, director-eligible/nonboss/nonscript-only flags. `encounterEligible` is opt-in to section eligibility; Charlie is explicitly rejected. No new random encounter spawns or story changes were invented. Visible/arena bounds gates and same-instance reentry are functional now; a future encounter director must consume this metadata and its own progression/difficulty/complexity limits. Full integration with absent global systems cannot be verified.
