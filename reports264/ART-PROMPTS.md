# Nightshade264 art provenance

Built-in image generation produced art-source264/counters.png using Nightshade's supplied design as reference: crisp detailed arcade pixel art, consistent teal/ivory/gold outfit, dark pants, approved proportions, physically readable counter catches, low takedown, kneeling hand taser and baton poses, no extra limbs, transparent separable cells, consistent lighting and pixel density.

Runtime imports only three new whole-body poses: cells 2, 4 and 6. Five other poses alias approved existing full-body artwork, and the handgun sequence reuses the established Gun255 art. tools/import-nightshade264.py records the fixed source aspect/presentation in reports264/art-import.json. New art is a candidate pending user approval; it is not certified by metadata or automated tests.
