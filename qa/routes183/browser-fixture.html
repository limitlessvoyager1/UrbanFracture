BROXTON / THE TURNCOAT

Heavy hands. A clean break.

A former syndicate enforcer fighting for the heroes. Slower than Reina, stronger at close range, and vulnerable during heavy windups. Broxton can grab active enemies and bosses at any health.

Strike & close distance

The opening punch must connect to continue. Attack: two jabs, a straight punch and a short rushing uppercut; the final hit staggers without automatically knocking down. Forward, forward + Attack: a committed running clothesline, with visible wind-up, multiple targets, knockdown and punishable recovery. Hold Attack 3s then release: Iron Will. Ten seconds of 50% ordinary attack damage and resistance to stagger, knockdown and grabs;75s cooldown starts when it ends. Hazards and explicitly scripted mechanics retain their rules. Jump + Attack: neutral heel kick or moving downward punch with brief stun. Air + down + Attack: diagonal elbow drop (16 damage).

Take control

Touch to grab. Attack: two headbutts, then a knockback uppercut. Each hit requires a separate press; choose a throw between hits. Front hold + back + Attack: full 360-degree spin with a backward throw. Front hold + forward + Attack: football tackle, mounted four-punch finish and standing recovery (26 damage, no special cost). Block or Dodge during the punches to dismount defensively. Rear hold + back + Attack: overhead backbreaker, then a short backward toss. Rear hold + forward + Attack: overhead forward throw into nearby enemies. Hold Jump/Grab Transition alone to raise a Human Shield (12-frame setup;2.5s maximum). Hold it with a direction to reposition up to one body-width without switching grab orientation. Release the shield button early for a zero-damage shove and short recovery. Front Grab + Special: one-handed chokeslam (23 HP),72 damage and extended knockdown. Committed throws are protected; their startup and recovery remain vulnerable.

Power has a price

Special: spinning punch clear-out (17 HP). Forward + Special: dash grab into jumping slam (23 HP), 47 damage, shorter reach and one-hit approach armor, with knockback around the impact and invincibility throughout. Whiffs still cost health; the forward special bypasses grab resistance. Airborne specials are disabled. L1 blocks. Timed R1 counters resolve automatically: against punches, forward tackles and neutral slams behind; against kicks, forward clotheslines and neutral tosses behind. A regular knife stab is caught, disarmed and countered with the knife. Other weapon attacks use an evade. Normal counters cost 40%; knife/heavy/boss counters cost 80%; heavies must be at 50% HP or less. Dodge costs 25% defense and uses a short boxing shuffle that switches sides with a nearby enemy; recover from the floor with a roll.

SKYLAR NOVA / HIGH-FLYING WRESTLER

Fast hands. Big air.

115 HP · fastest walk · higher jump. Direction is relative to the way Skylar faces.

Strikes and flight

Attack: five-hit combo, 29 total damage. The opening hit must connect to continue. Double-tap forward + Attack: handstand, flips and splits. Hold Attack 2s to automatically enter Hype Mode: ten seconds of strength, speed and armor, plus 20% health recovery. Unlimited activations in this test build. Jump + Attack: neutral kick (11 damage) or moving dropkick (14 damage). Down + Attack in the air: cannonball drop (13 damage).

Wrestling grabs

Front grab + Attack: two knees and a head-to-knee strike, 28 total damage. Forward: tornado DDT. Back: belly-to-belly suplex and flip follow-up, 29 total damage. Rear grab + forward: face smash. Rear grab + back: Frankensteiner. Grab + Special: Exploding Nova (18 HP): skyward signal, shoulder launch, corkscrew and body splash. Its pressure rings deal zero damage and keep nearby enemies from advancing until Nova recovers. Bruno needs ≤60% HP, Brick ≤25%; Jackal and other bosses are excluded.

Specials and counters

Special: splits clearout, 18 damage (12 HP). Forward + Special: rushing ten-hit combination, 40 total damage, ending in a double dropkick and backflip (18 HP). Punch counter: neutral catches into a stronger grab; forward throws behind. Kick counter: neutral stomp follow-up; forward jumping kick. Airborne specials remain disabled.

FOREST / THE HUNTER & GUNNER

Work as a pack.

120 HP · methodical power and range. Gunner follows automatically, never blocks movement and has no health bar. R1 counters use latched direction and timed interceptions.

Knife, then crossbow

The opening knife slash must connect before the combo can continue. Six separate Attack presses: three knife slashes, then three crossbow bolts with increasing knockback (36 total damage). Forward, forward + Attack: advancing slashes into Gunner’s tackle. Hold Attack 2 seconds, then release to command Gunner Maul. Jump + Attack: downward swipe; moving jump: heel drop; down + Attack in air: knife stab.

Restrain and finish

Grab + Attack: three individual knees, then a spinning knife finisher. Choose a directional throw between knees. Front away: arch throw + Gunner; front forward: spinebuster + kneedrop. Rear forward: restraint + Gunner; rear away: back-to-back flip, Gunner and two knife strikes. Jump changes grab positions.

Control the crowd

Special: two front knife swipes and three rear bites (16 HP). Forward + Special: five straight piercing bolts (20 HP). Grab + Special: five knife hits into a hard takedown (20 HP). L1 blocks, ○ dodges; R1: forward punch counter (30% defense), neutral punch counter (35%), forward kick + Gunner (35%), neutral kick + crossbow (40%), neutral weapon disarm (50%). Failures cost 15%. Strong enemies need ≤60% HP; eligible bosses also require full defense. Gunner can be targeted and temporarily stunned. At low health he can intercept one attacker or guard an enemy knockdown, and below half health he retrieves nearby health items. Automatic abilities share a cooldown; commanded attacks remain separate. A completed Maul charge is held while Gunner recovers.
