## Selection and acceptance

Only a clean, valid QA-only pilot declaration can propose the replacement. The impact plan preserves its original selection and records the exact contract hash, loss-receipt hash, mappings and unverified claims. The four historical rows are Not Tested in this new pilot execution, explicitly labelled historical evidence unavailable; they are never Passed. Their historical evidence status remains Blocked in historyReplacement, and frozen original Failed receipts remain visible in Git and the uploaded artifact.

The new four suites must all execute successfully. Required new failures, unavailable tools, skipped checks, changed source or malformed mapping block automation. All unrelated required suites remain required. Full release selects the original historical suites and remains subject to existing manual, source, provenance and certification gates. The contract does not change certifications.json or performance-baselines.json.

Passing automation produces reviewable pilot evidence, not accepted policy or game approval. The owner job checks the authenticated repository owner's latest exact comment against current PR/base/head and the immutable evidence digest. Ordinary earlier pilot comments cannot acknowledge this contract. A new source, contract or evidence digest requires new signoff; REVOKE/malformed/stale comments block. No tool posts the comment for the owner, merges, deploys or enables protection.
