# Reina air-strike hair, kip-up and katana correction

The owner's November 17 reports identify a remaining ponytail in the airborne down + attack sword strike and German-throw kip-up. The owner also supplied a katana reference and requested a slight visual blade shortening. The replacement keeps loose navy hair, a slender single-edged curved blade, an angled tip, a small guard and a wrapped grip. The blade shortening target is approximately 5%; this is an artistic target, not a change to collision reach.

The active downward-strike art is supplied by Katana355. Its full-body substitutions replace the earlier Reina354 rare canvases. Editing only the earlier canvases leaves the old hairstyle visible; bypassing Katana355 also changes the established body and sword presentation. The final implementation therefore adds a separate replacement katana atlas for exactly eight dive poses and retains the existing renderer, source rectangles, body placement, original sheets and all unrelated poses. Four replacement kip-up frames use their original native anchors and scale. Normal grounded recovery already uses the loose-hair Reina383 atlas and remains unchanged.

## Verification and limits

The current native baseline is preserved separately from historical evidence. Twelve traces cover 30/60/120 Hz and both directions for the downward strike and German throw. The test compares positions, vertical movement, action clocks, hit timing, health, defense, enemy fall state and RNG exactly. No combat or input source is changed by this correction. The approved earlier 10% slower dive descent remains in place.

The original active katana atlas is 1254 by 1254 pixels: its four-by-four cells have fractional 313.5-pixel extents. The replacement uses a 1280 by 1280 atlas with exact 320-pixel cells. The importer normalizes the original sampled cells to that grid before matching body landmarks. The existing destination scale, grip coordinates and pose clocks stay unchanged. The independent native audit uses the same physically equivalent normalization when comparing original and replacement art. It retains the 0.70 raw anatomy-mask overlap, 0.97–1.04 body width ratio and one-unit head/foot registration limits. One-pixel overlap is recorded as a diagnostic, not a substitute pass criterion. Earlier failed comparisons and the superseded pre-katana route receipt remain visible. The low-resolution katana foot probe uses the toe columns plus one pixel; a three-pixel expansion also included the old blade tip in dive/4 and wrongly reported the approved blade change as a 50-unit body shift. The side-by-side decoded frames show the feet aligned. The higher-resolution kip probe retains its three-pixel support margin. No registration limit is relaxed. Artistic approval remains a separate manual gate.

The native render audit must observe all eight active katana frames and all four kip frames in both facings, with no native-state or RNG mutation during rendering. It writes source hashes, decoded registration measurements and contact sheets. Asset integrity and untouched normal-recovery/source references are checked independently.
