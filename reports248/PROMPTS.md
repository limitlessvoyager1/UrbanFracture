The first L output mirrored those materials incorrectly and was rejected. Final targeted correction: replace near-side silver forearms/hands with human skin emerging from coat cuffs, replace far-side skin hands with robotic metal, make the near left cheek human, retain all poses/hand coordinates/layout and transparent backdrop.

Import operations are technical atlas cropping, one fixed per-hero reduction to native pixel density, sole/hand-anchor metadata and binary pixel-art alpha. No per-animation dynamic resizing and no changes to approved existing art. Weapon silhouettes remain the game's code-native shapes so a stolen crowbar, pipe, bat, plank, baton or scalpel retains its actual category and visible object instead of being baked as one generic pipe.
