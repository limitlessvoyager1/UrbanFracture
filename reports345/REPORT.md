## Limits / remaining work
Foreign dropped-weapon pickup remains disabled by the inherited344 drone prototype; the original game is unaffected.

This is a playable review prototype, not a completed visual overhaul or stable release. The new18-pose enemy templates do not yet author every existing chain/taser/knife/martial/boss move. Complete move-specific anticipation/contact/recovery and paired hand placement remain to inspect and improve. Other legacy Nightshade grapples are not replaced by the rolling throw work. Smooth interpolation alone is not treated as authored anatomy.

The optional arenas have native simulation proof, but their full player-traversed entry/fight/reward/exit chains have not all been recorded in the browser. Window pickup reach and all main-route transitions need the same end-to-end browser coverage. No full before/after recording, all-enemy/all-move artistic approval, performance certification or physical DualSense result is claimed. Drone mode persistence and full new-move Developer Panel adapters remain open. Existing performance/release holds remain open; main-game publication stays held.

## Distribution
Downtown-only review ZIP includes launchers and README. Unzip fully. With Python3 installed, run START-WINDOWS.bat, then open http://127.0.0.1:8000/ in Chrome/Edge on the user's computer. macOS/Linux: sh START-MAC-LINUX.sh. Connect DualSense to that computer and press a face button, then Options. A cloud browser cannot see a controller connected to the user's computer. Do not open index with file://.
