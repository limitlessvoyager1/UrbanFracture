MAIN FINDING
A clean contact receipt followed by flight is valid history, not evidence that recoil or knockdown failed. Niko palms resolves damage first, then launches the surviving target. Current physical state correctly becomes fall, knockdown, get-up and recovery while the old contact outcome remains clean.

Confirmed problems are in diagnostic presentation/correlation: playback's outer phase says actionable during flight; and the zero-damage transfer inside launch emits a second contact-feedback receipt, replacing the latest feedback damage value with null. No duplicated HP loss was observed. The metric actualSeconds=0 accurately reports stun clearing in the launch tick under its implementation, but is ambiguous if read as completed reaction/recovery.

RECOMMENDATION
First approve NL-01: a small read-only diagnostic projection/label repair distinguishing last contact outcome, current physical state and observed stun-clear elapsed time. Preserve raw contact outcomes and legacy timing fields. Keep NL-02 (zero-damage feedback duplication) separate because filtering feedback requires validating intentional zero-damage contacts. Do not rewrite Niko's move, flight physics, recovery clocks, or the wrapper stack.

CONSUMER REVIEW
The receipt is not globally immutable: its timing fields are completed later, and unrelated weapon/Nightshade modules can extend durationAtHit. This proposal preserves that existing behavior and separates meanings rather than assuming every receipt field is a timeless snapshot.
-Contact302 reads contemporaneous reaction outcome to classify contact feedback; armor/block must not be overwritten by later movement state.
-Reactions-view298 requires actual remaining stun and excludes flight/down/jump before applying recoil offset. ReactionTemplates289 and enemy-reactions290 prioritize physical flight/down before standing recoil. No evidence here that clean history forces standing recoil over flight.
-Playback265 and HitReactions298.report expose outcome beside live stun lock/elapsed values without clearly distinct contact/state semantics. HitReactions298.locked is a stun/shock guard, not a full-recovery guard; false during flight is not itself a bypass.
-recording312-enemies reads contemporaneous armor outcome for thrown-projectile impacts; recording313/recording313-enemies and nightshade-melee257 intentionally adjust their own receipt-related states. Nova view uses sanitized copies. PCBI view reads a separate configuredSeconds field/fallback while projecting its own states. No changes to these unrelated consumers are proposed.
