# URBAN FRACTURE — JESSE COMPLETE ENEMY COMBAT & AI OVERHAUL
## Build 205 | Gunfighter AI, Unblockable Firearms, Jump Evasion, Reload Vulnerability, and Coordinated Gunfire

### PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Refine Jesse into a low-HP, high-damage ranged enemy whose primary objective is to maintain distance and fire his weapon.

Jesse should be:

- Extremely dangerous when left unattended.
- Vulnerable when approached successfully.
- Dependent on gunfire rather than advanced melee techniques.
- Capable of repositioning and occasional defensive rolls.
- Vulnerable while reloading.
- Dangerous in groups without producing unavoidable gunfire.

**Jesse's gunshots are unblockable, but they must be avoidable through skillful timing, movement, dodging, jumping, or legitimate environmental cover.**

Preserve the existing six-shot magazine and established encounter placements.

Create a reversible checkpoint before implementation.

Implement three engagement zones using actual combat ranges.

### Close range

Jesse prefers:

- Knife Stab.
- Short retreat.
- Repositioning.
- Defensive movement after completing recovery.

### Preferred firearm range

Jesse prefers:

- Aiming.
- Firing.

### Outside effective firearm range

### Important

# PHASE 4 — FIREARM AIMING

### Initial startup

30–36 frames at 60 FPS for initial target acquisition.

### Animation

1. Jesse notices the player.
2. He raises his firearm.
3. He turns toward the target.
4. He plants his feet.
5. He establishes a firing stance.
6. A readable aiming cue appears.
7. He commits to the shot.
8. The firearm discharges.

### Requirements

- Visible gun-raising animation.
- Clear muzzle direction.
- Correct firing-hand attachment.
- No instant firing from idle.
- No projectile before the release event.
- No automatic perfect tracking.

### Important

The aiming cue must be readable even when other enemies are onscreen.

---

# PHASE 5 — FIRING ANIMATION

Each gunshot must have:

- A defined firing event.
- Muzzle flash.
- Appropriate sound.
- Recoil animation.
- Projectile or hitscan resolution according to the existing weapon framework.
- Post-shot recovery.

### Initial timing

- First shot: After initial 30–36-frame aim.
- Subsequent shot preparation: 12–16 frames.
- Post-shot recovery: 18–22 frames.

Do not double-count preparation and recovery if the existing state machine combines them.

### Important

A six-round magazine does not mean Jesse automatically fires six shots in one uninterrupted burst.

He may reposition or reassess between shots.

---

# PHASE 6 — GUNSHOT COLLISION AND BLOCKING

### Blocking

Gunfire cannot be blocked by ordinary hero guarding.

### Evasion

Gunfire may miss if:

- The player dodges during valid evasive frames.
- The player's airborne hurtbox clears the shot trajectory.
- The player moves outside the firing lane before discharge.
- Solid cover blocks the shot.
- Jesse misses because the target moved after aim commitment.

### Requirements

- No homing bullets.
- No invisible full-screen hitboxes.
- No automatic damage merely because Jesse fired.
- No damage through valid solid cover.
- No duplicate damage from one shot.
- No bypassing valid dodge invulnerability.

### Important

If the existing gun system uses hitscan, resolve collision at the firing event against the target's actual current hurtbox and defensive state.

Do not silently replace hitscan with projectile simulation unless necessary.

---

# PHASE 7 — AIM COMMITMENT AND FAIRNESS

Jesse may track the player during early aiming.

However, he must lock his firing direction or target lane during the final committed aiming phase.

### Initial target

Lock the shot approximately 10–12 frames before firing.

### Requirements

- The player can recognize the firing lane.
- Moving out of that lane can avoid the shot.
- Jesse cannot instantly retarget at the muzzle-flash frame.
- The aiming animation remains synchronized with the actual trajectory.

### Important

Do not make every shot perfectly accurate.

A player who correctly changes position should sometimes cause Jesse to miss.

# PHASE 8 — SIX-SHOT MAGAZINE

Jesse begins with six rounds.

After each shot:

1. Decrease ammunition by one.
2. Update the firearm state.
3. Continue ordinary combat if ammunition remains.
4. Begin reload when the magazine is empty.

### Important

Do not generate unlimited shots without reloading.

Do not allow ammunition to reset through AI state changes.

---

# PHASE 9 — RELOADING

### Initial duration

90–120 frames at 60 FPS.

### Animation

1. Jesse lowers his firearm.
2. He removes or accesses the magazine.
3. He inserts a fresh magazine.
4. He completes the weapon-ready motion.
5. He returns to an aiming-capable state.

### Vulnerability

During reload:

- No firearm attacks.
- No firearm armor.
- No automatic guard.
- No instant dodge cancel.
- No automatic knife attack.
- Normal damage and stagger apply.

### Important

Reloading is Jesse's principal punish window.

Do not allow the AI Director to skip it.

If the reload is interrupted, resume or restart according to a consistent documented rule; never silently refill ammunition.

A simple defensive attack when the player reaches close range.

### Initial frame data

- Startup: 14–17 frames.
- Active: 4 frames.
- Recovery: 24–28 frames.

### Animation

1. Jesse draws or raises his knife.
2. He takes a short forward step.
3. He thrusts the knife.
4. He retracts his arm.
5. He returns to stance.

### Properties

- Short range.
- Moderate damage.
- No armor.
- No elaborate combinations.
- No automatic knockdown.
- Eligible for existing weapon counters and disarms.

### Important

Jesse must not become as capable in close combat as Kevin.

# PHASE 11 — KNIFE-TO-RETREAT BEHAVIOR

After completing the Knife Stab:

- Jesse may attempt to retreat.
- Retreat begins only after mandatory recovery.
- Movement must use visible footwork.
- Retreat cannot pass through walls.
- Retreat must respect combat-plane boundaries.

### Important

A successful knife stab does not guarantee escape.

### Initial probabilities

| Remaining HP | Roll chance |
|---|---:|
| Above 50% | 15% |
| 25–50% | 25% |
| Below 25% | 40% |

### Requirements

- Respect minimum grounded duration.
- Do not roll during synchronized throws.
- Do not interrupt the hero's mandatory throw animation.
- Roll away from the player's position.
- Require a valid escape path.
- Use a visible rolling animation.
- No teleportation.
- No instant firing after rolling.

### Distance

Approximately 0.75–1 character-width.

### Important

# PHASE 13 — RECOVERY ROLL FAIRNESS

A recovery roll must not become a free escape from every knockdown.

### Restrictions

- No unlimited invulnerability.
- No immediate second roll.
- No automatic gunshot during roll recovery.
- No bypass of grab-only anti-regrab protection.
- No rolling through solid scenery.
- No roll when the escape path is blocked.

Preserve the shared post-throw fairness rule.

If Jesse is knocked down by a synchronized throw, the hero must regain the established defensive opportunity before Jesse can execute an offensive attack.
