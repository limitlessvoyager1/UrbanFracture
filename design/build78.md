Prompt: preserve each pose, identity, scale, layout and pixel-art style; keep the red top and yellow-white exploding-star design; make the wrestling top resemble the reference, recolor gloves and boots pale star-colored yellow-white, and make the boot laces red.

Assets: `dist/assets/sprites/nova-motion78.png`, `nova-attacks78.png`, `nova-grapple78.png`, `nova-acrobat78.png`, and `dist/assets/nova-portrait78.png`. Sheet dimensions and frame locations remain matched to the existing animation definitions.
