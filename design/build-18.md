# Build 18 — Velvet Room scene transition, furniture and anti-air

Outside now ends at a dedicated broad Velvet Room facade with a visible marquee and closed entrance doors. After the last nightlife enemy is defeated, player input locks briefly and Reina walks to the entrance automatically, steps into the doorway, fades from view, and the screen fades to black. The scene swaps only at full black, then fades back in to the separate interior. Camera limits prevent the room being revealed beside the outside street. The rear service exit uses the same fade sequence after the venue fight and leads into Jackal's alley. Checkpoint healing, retry and one-second arrival protection are preserved. Scene transitions clear old enemies/items/props, reset input and spawn the next encounter once.

Widened exterior district spans from 1080 to 1680 world units so the existing panoramic facades render close to their natural aspect ratio rather than being squeezed. The route remains seven encounters and 18 enemies, over 11400 units. Added a separate interior background with a completely clear playable floor; fixed bar stools, stage, performers and kitchen remain behind the combat floor. Removed the continuous exterior/interior cutaway columns.

Six illustrated tables/chairs are independent props with floor collision footprints, shared depth sorting with actors, upright and toppled sprites, and .32-second tipping motion. Walking into a table blocks Reina first, then sustained pressure tips it (.34 seconds; chairs .18). Running or an attacking collision topples immediately; normal attacks can also knock furniture over. Tipped furniture no longer blocks. Rear furniture renders behind Reina and nearer furniture in front. Painted foreground dining furniture was removed from the new background, preventing the walking-on-tables effect.

Bruno anti-air: rising uppercut, 18 damage, .21 windup. Frankie: upward chain swing, 16 damage, .25 windup, 160 range. Jackal: rising swing using his current bat/pipe/knife, 18/20/17 damage, .21 windup, 130 range. They react to incoming jumps in the same depth lane, only when free and off cooldown. A short committed portion resists interruption by airborne attacks; grounded attacks still interrupt. Active window .2 seconds, one hit maximum, knockdown, about one-second total action plus recovery and a 1.85-second anti-air cooldown. Off-lane jumps avoid the trigger. All have dedicated art; Jackal's artwork matches his equipped weapon.
