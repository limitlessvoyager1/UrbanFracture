# Build 46 — readable kicks, counters and grounded movement

Reina's neutral aerial attack is now a downward heel kick, with three dedicated poses, a sustained 0.10–0.42-second contact window, 110px forward reach, limited coverage directly below/slightly behind her root, and 32px lane tolerance. It retains 20 base damage and one hit per enemy. Moving kick remains separate. Her front grab finisher is a clenched downward hammer punch (two knees plus finisher still total 23 damage) with dedicated windup/impact/recovery art.

Saito and Akari use one-foot flying kicks, a travel/contact window instead of a single remote hit test, a continuous arc that reaches ground at 0.54 seconds, and a short grounded landing recovery. Both can be caught by Reina's forward/neutral counters; caught enemies lose their airborne state. Combo kicks occur closer together, with a modest step toward the target, visible kick-number synchronization, and a longer recovery between attack sequences. Flying kicks have a longer four-second selection cooldown. Their guard now uses an explicit block action and a visible retaliation after repeated blocked attacks; they no longer silently block while attacking or from behind. Paid specials bypass their guard. Saito's kip-up and Akari's wake sweep remain.

Walking uses opaque textured two-bone legs, stationary body proportions, feet planted flat during contact, and alternating passing steps. It replaces the old dissolving two-pose specialist walk and Carmen's crouched/toe gait. Carmen's actor scale is 9% larger overall and the walk atlas matches standing height. Velvet Room patrons are reduced from 0.36 to 0.28 seated scale; escaping patrons now use alternating running frames at 0.24 scale with a movement phase tied to their escape. Marcus retains randomized temperaments, with fewer aggressive rolls, reduced pressure acceleration and longer pauses between opportunistic commitments.
