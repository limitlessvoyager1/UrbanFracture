# Build 45 — combat and presentation fixes

Reina's aerial down+Attack knee deals 10 base damage. Her neutral aerial attack uses three new spinning roundhouse poses at a fixed 0.25 atlas scale; forward kick remains separate. A new front grapple pose separates the high and low grips. The neutral punch counter now throws behind; forward still performs the three-hit palm combo. Kick counters retain forward forearm and neutral rear grip. Away has no separate move and falls back to the neutral response. Knife counters only intercept regular stab actions (knife/robberStab), not slashes, hyper slashes, throws or rushing weapon swings. They visibly capture the arm then flip and disarm the target using dedicated poses and a synchronized victim arc. Existing meter costs, timing, lockout and heavy health gates remain.

Bruno approaches within the visible camera arena instead of the old district boundary, with a modestly faster approach. Kevin retains stab, a longer step-in slash, and now 850px/sec knife throws. At 35% HP or lower he uses a single three-hit advancing hyper slash, without a grab/stab hold. Carmen's leap is no longer selected by AI: she instead aims and fires paired wired taser probes. Hits produce the existing shocked pose/stun; lane movement and jumping evade the probes. She occasionally uses a cooldown-limited short dash to close distance. Her articulated walking legs now use opaque uniform segments, eliminating sampled baton/white fragments; the baton shine that crossed her leg was removed.
