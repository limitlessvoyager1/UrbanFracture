# Slums art generation / build 202

Generated using the built-in image-generation tool, with a detailed crisp 16-bit Streets of Rage–inspired visual brief matching Urban Fracture. Original generated PNGs are retained here; runtime WebPs live in dist/assets. No existing character design was regenerated.

- **street.png** → slums-street202.webp. Unique wide panorama: wooded road entrance at left, rundown homes/corner shop, torn KEEP OUT fences, rusted stop/street signs, broken poles and debris; apartment entrance upper far right and department store partly visible lower right. Open asphalt combat lane in bottom30%, sidewalk near70%, red smoky distant skyline. Broken amber-lit windows. Exclude people, cars, large flames and smoke plumes for runtime placement. 2172×724.
- **floors.png** → slums-floors202.webp. Three equal horizontal strips: ruined lobby with OUT OF ORDER elevator and right stairs; upper corridor with left stairs and smoky windows; another ruined corridor with right stairs, burnt utility closet and exposed beams. Consistent floor/camera and building proportions. Exclude people, lane props, holes and fire for runtime. 2172×724.
- **store-roof.png** → slums-store-roof202.webp. Two equal horizontal strips: looted department store with clothing racks, empty checkouts, mannequins, boarded windows and service stairs right; tar apartment rooftop with left stair door, water tank, vents, dishes, skyline, woods and mountains beneath red smoke. Open foreground combat lanes. Exclude people/cars/flames/UI. 2172×724.
- **crowd.png** → slums-crowd202.webp. Transparent4×3 atlas, twelve isolated full-body frames. Row1 older gaunt homeless man, long gray beard, dirty brown coat, patched trousers and worn shoes: worried idle, two planted walk steps, sitting under blanket. Row2 Black middle-aged woman, dirty green sweater, faded trousers/sneakers and cloth bag: idle, two grounded running steps, crouch. Row3 young stubbled man, ragged red hoodie/jeans and dirty shoes: idle, two hurried steps, slumped blanket sitting. Respectful somber tone, opaque bodies, transparent background, no floor/shadows/text, consistent standing height. 1448×1086. Runtime normalizes opaque body alpha and retains source anatomy and frame scale.

The shared existing fire atlas, emergency vehicles, and native Marcus/Kevin/Frankie/Carmen scenery/combat art are reused. No real-person imagery.
