# Build 04 — contact grabs and walking

Walking into a regular thug in the same lane acquires a front or rear hold immediately. No grab button is required. Basic thugs can be caught during attack wind-up; committed active attacks remain dangerous. Armed basic thugs follow the same contact-grab rule. Heavy/boss grabs still require stagger. Manual G grab remains optional; G also releases a hold. Holding attack prioritizes the combo rather than automatically grabbing. Jumping, rolling, knockdown, active attacks, and another hold cannot trigger contact acquisition. Release immunity prevents immediate re-grab loops.

Normal thugs have no committed-attack armor: successful hits cancel their moves and stagger them. When a normal thug hits an attacking Reina, she loses health and receives normal hit invulnerability, but her attack continues without stagger or knockdown. Enforcers and the leader can interrupt her and retain limited armor during committed heavy swings; armor does not prevent damage.

Each character now has an additional generated eight-frame walking atlas, under dist/assets/sprites/*-walk-v1.png. Identity and outfit follow the existing concepts. Image-generation briefs requested consecutive contact/loading/passing/swing phases in a transparent 4x2 layout. Connected-alpha analysis supplies frame bounds and stable torso/foot anchors in walk-data.js. Animation advances by simulated distance traveled and pauses when the actor stops. Walking does not alternate with idle poses. Armed walking has a separate attached weapon overlay. The Sprite Room's Walk option uses the same walking renderer.

Validation: 18 existing combat regressions, contact-grab/poise regressions (including live ordinary AI wind-up), 32 walk-frame bounds and phase-selection checks, existing sprite-state checks, and an ordinary-input complete level simulation. Browser confirmed a front grab from a rightward walk without G, and reviewed the dedicated walking previews. No application runtime errors observed.
