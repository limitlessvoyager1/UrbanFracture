# URBAN FRACTURE — AKARI COMPLETE COMBAT, AI & ENCOUNTER OVERHAUL
## Build 205 | Fast Adaptive Martial Artist | Normal Difficulty

### PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Refine Akari into a fast, unpredictable martial-arts specialist who alternates between assertive offense, disciplined defense, and deceptive pauses.

Akari shares some of Saito's combat abilities but must feel substantially different.

**Saito controls the pace through patience and intimidation. Akari controls the pace through sudden aggression, speed, and changes in rhythm.**

Implement:

1. Faster, lower-damage martial-arts attacks.
2. Three distinct tactical behavior modes.
3. Rapid three-kick combination.
4. Advancing flying kick.
5. Punch, kick, and grab counters.
6. Occasional Blitz interception.
7. High-kick anti-air.
8. Evasion against eligible unblockable specials.
9. Feigned recovery traps.
10. Momentum-based tactical adaptation.
11. Quick defensive sidesteps.
12. Conditional recovery sweep and kip-up.
13. Fair recovery after player throws.
14. Coordinated behavior with other enemies.
15. Gradual campaign encounter progression for Akari and Saito.

Preserve Akari's existing visual identity, established HP, spawn references, and functioning animations.

Do not automatically increase her HP or damage.

Create a reversible checkpoint before implementation.

# PART 2 — CHARACTER IDENTITY

Akari is an assertive, agile martial artist with strong defensive knowledge but less patience and discipline than Saito.

### Strengths

- Fast movement.
- Quick attack startup.
- Shorter recovery than Saito.
- Rapid kick combinations.
- Strong defensive reactions.
- Quick sidesteps.
- Occasional deceptive counter baiting.
- Faster transitions between tactical approaches.
- Ability to pressure players in short bursts.

### Weaknesses

- Lower HP than Saito.
- Lower damage than Saito.
- No attack armor.
- Vulnerable to grabs.
- Vulnerable to successful player counters.
- Punishable missed flying kicks.
- Punishable genuine recovery.
- Limited defensive cooldowns.
- Can overcommit during aggressive phases.

### Critical requirement

Akari must not become a faster version of Saito with identical animations.

Her attacks must look different while preserving comparable tactical effects.

# PART 3 — THREE TACTICAL AI MODES

Implement three temporary tactical modes.

## ASSERTIVE MODE

Akari aggressively closes distance and pressures the player.

### Behavior

- Advances quickly.
- Favors rapid kick combinations.
- Occasionally uses flying kick.
- Pursues vulnerable opponents.
- Attempts to maintain offensive momentum.
- Less likely to guard.
- More willing to overcommit.

### Initial duration

2–4 seconds.

## DEFENSIVE MODE

Akari prioritizes survival and counter opportunities.

### Behavior

- Guards eligible frontal attacks.
- Sidesteps predictable strikes.
- Attempts appropriate counters.
- Repositions.
- Waits for attack openings.
- Reduces unnecessary offensive commitments.

### Initial duration

2–3 seconds.

## DECEPTIVE MODE

Akari temporarily changes rhythm to bait a player response.

### Behavior

- Abruptly stops advancing.
- Adopts a seemingly vulnerable stance.
- Occasionally uses feigned recovery.
- Watches for observable player attacks.
- Attempts a legitimate timed counter when eligible.

### Initial duration

0.6–1.2 seconds.

### Important

Do not switch tactical modes during committed attacks, synchronized interactions, mandatory recovery, or knockdown.

Mode changes occur at valid action boundaries.

# PART 5 — MOMENTUM SYSTEM

Implement a hidden tactical Momentum variable.

### Momentum increases when:

- Akari lands attacks.
- She forces the player to retreat.
- She successfully maintains pressure.
- She creates an advantageous position.

### Momentum decreases when:

- The player counters her.
- The player successfully throws her.
- She misses a committed attack.
- She is repeatedly interrupted.

### High Momentum

- Increased Assertive priority.
- More frequent eligible kick combinations.
- Greater willingness to approach.
- Reduced defensive hesitation.
- Greater risk of overcommitment.

### Low Momentum

- Increased Defensive priority.
- More repositioning.
- Occasional deceptive baiting.
- Reduced reckless flying-kick selection.

### Important

Momentum affects decision-making only.

Do not increase attack damage, movement speed, armor, or attack speed through Momentum.

Momentum should gradually return toward neutral over time.

# PART 17 — FEIGNED RECOVERY
## SIGNATURE DECEPTIVE MECHANIC

### Purpose

Bait players into attacking when Akari appears temporarily vulnerable.

### Activation

Only when Akari is already actionable.

### Sequence

1. Akari abruptly stops advancing or attacking.
2. She adopts a slightly off-balance pose.
3. She appears to be recovering.
4. She waits briefly for an observable player response.
5. If the player initiates an eligible attack, Akari may attempt a timed counter.
6. If no attack occurs, she returns to normal combat behavior.

### Initial duration

0.6–1.0 seconds.

### Cooldown

10–14 seconds.

### Critical requirement

**Never cancel genuine mandatory attack recovery into Feigned Recovery.**

### Visual distinction

Genuine recovery:

- Weight visibly committed.
- Feet poorly positioned.
- No defensive action available.

Feigned recovery:

- Rear foot remains planted.
- Shoulders remain prepared.
- Subtle readiness cue.

### Failure

If the bait fails or Akari commits to an unsuccessful counter, she must enter appropriate vulnerable recovery.

### Important

Not every apparent opening should be deceptive.

Genuine missed attacks must remain punishable.
