# URBAN FRACTURE — SAITO COMPLETE ENEMY COMBAT & AI OVERHAUL
## Build 205 | Defensive Martial Artist | Consolidated Implementation Specification | Normal Difficulty

### PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Refine Saito into an intelligent, highly defensive martial-arts specialist who severely punishes predictable aggression.

Saito's combat identity must emphasize:

1. Intimidation through deliberate movement and patience.
2. Strong frontal blocking with chip damage.
3. High-damage counters against punches and kicks.
4. Occasional grab reversals.
5. A powerful three-kick combination.
6. A medium-range flying kick.
7. An intercepting kick against eligible Blitz attacks.
8. A rising high-kick anti-air.
9. Intelligent evasion against unblockable specials.
10. Faster kip-up recovery.
11. A hidden Defensive Composure system.
12. Tactical adaptation to repeated player behavior.
13. Punishable mistakes and meaningful weaknesses.

**Saito should punish careless aggression, not automatically defeat every attack.**

His weaknesses must remain:

- Vulnerability to legitimate grabs.
- Vulnerability to attacks from behind.
- No attack armor.
- Punishable missed counters.
- Punishable kick combinations.
- Punishable flying kicks.
- Limited defensive cooldowns.
- No universal defense against unblockable specials.

Preserve existing playable heroes, enemies, levels, environmental systems, and controls.

Create a reversible checkpoint before implementation.

# PHASE 2 — CHARACTER IDENTITY

Saito is a disciplined, confident martial artist who prefers to control the pace of a fight.

He should appear calm and intimidating.

### Strengths

- Excellent frontal defense.
- Powerful counters.
- Strong close-range kicking.
- Good anticipation of predictable attacks.
- Faster knockdown recovery.
- Controlled movement.
- Strong punishment against reckless aggression.

### Weaknesses

- Grab attacks.
- Attacks from behind.
- Missed counter recovery.
- Missed flying-kick recovery.
- Interrupted kick combinations.
- Limited defense against unblockable specials.
- Reduced defensive effectiveness after losing Composure.

### Important

Do not give Saito permanent armor, automatic invulnerability, or perfect reaction timing.

# PHASE 3 — INTIMIDATION WALK

## Primary AI behavior

Saito should frequently approach the player without immediately attacking.

### Animation

1. Saito faces the player.
2. He walks forward slowly and confidently.
3. He maintains a relaxed martial-arts guard.
4. His steps remain deliberate.
5. He stops near threatening range.
6. He observes the player.
7. He may attack, guard, counter, reposition, or continue walking.

### Important

Saito must not constantly rush toward the player.

His controlled advance should communicate confidence.

### Movement requirements

- Natural foot placement.
- No sliding.
- No teleportation.
- Consistent sprite scale.
- Correct combat-plane alignment.

# PHASE 4 — INTIMIDATION PAUSE

Saito occasionally stops within threatening distance and does nothing.

### Initial duration

0.6–1.2 seconds.

Allow slight variation.

### Purpose

Bait impatient players into initiating attacks.

### Possible outcomes

- Continue standing.
- Raise guard.
- Prepare a counter.
- Begin a kick combination.
- Advance again.
- Reposition.

### Important

The pause must not secretly grant automatic counter success.

Saito must still satisfy normal defensive eligibility.

Do not allow indefinite inactivity.

When Saito is actionable at close-to-medium range:

| Action | Initial relative weight |
|---|---:|
| Controlled advance | 25% |
| Intimidation pause | 25% |
| Guard preparation | 20% |
| Three-kick combination | 20% |

### Important

Incoming threats use a separate defensive-response evaluation.

Do not let neutral decision-making prevent legitimate counter or anti-air opportunities.

If the player remains passive for too long, gradually increase Saito's willingness to initiate offense.

# PHASE 6 — FRONTAL BLOCKING

Saito should be substantially more effective at blocking than ordinary enemies.

### Initial Normal difficulty probabilities

| Situation | Guard attempt chance |
|---|---:|
| Eligible ordinary frontal attack | 35–45% |
| Repeated predictable attack string | 50–60% |
| Attack from behind | 0% |
| Mandatory recovery | 0% |
| Knockdown or stun | 0% |

### Blocking behavior

1. Recognize an observable incoming attack.
2. Verify Saito is actionable.
3. Verify attack eligibility.
4. Raise guard with a visible animation.
5. Resolve incoming contact through the existing block system.
6. Apply chip damage and appropriate guard pressure.
7. Return to stance or continue guarding briefly.

### Requirements

- Blocking is directional.
- Blocking consumes or stresses the existing guard resource where applicable.
- No blocking attacks from behind.
- No instant guard during recovery.
- No blocking explicitly unblockable attacks.
- No automatic counter after every successful block.

### Important

Saito must still take chip damage when blocking eligible attacks.

# PHASE 7 — COUNTER SYSTEM

Saito should use a dedicated defensive counter-selection layer built on the existing combat system.

### Initial probabilities

| Situation | Counter attempt chance |
|---|---:|
| Eligible ordinary punch or kick | 20–25% |
| Repeated predictable attack | 30–35% |
| Eligible frontal grab attempt | 10–15% |

These probabilities are evaluated only when the counter is eligible.

### Restrictions

- No counters from behind.
- No counters during recovery.
- No counters while stunned.
- No counters while knocked down.
- No counters during committed attacks.
- No counters against explicitly uncounterable moves.
- No automatic counter success based solely on probability.

### Important

The AI first decides whether to attempt a counter.

The counter must then succeed through actual timing and collision eligibility.

# PHASE 8 — PUNCH COUNTER

### Trigger

An eligible frontal punch approaches while Saito is actionable.

### Animation

1. Saito shifts slightly off the punch line.
2. He catches or redirects the attacking wrist.
3. He controls the opponent's arm.
4. He rotates his hips.
5. He drives a powerful palm strike into the hero's torso.
6. The hero staggers backward.
7. Saito releases the arm.
8. He returns to stance.

### Properties

- High counter damage.
- Strong standing stagger.
- Moderate pushback.
- No automatic knockdown unless the existing counter definition explicitly permits it.

### Requirements

- Continuous hand-to-arm contact.
- Correct synchronized animation.
- No teleportation.
- No invisible counter damage.
- Punishable failure.

### Requirements

# PHASE 17 — DEFENSE AGAINST UNBLOCKABLE SPECIALS

## Core rule

**Saito must not block specials that are explicitly unblockable.**

He should respond through positioning, evasion, or legitimate startup interruption.

### Available responses

#### Sidestep

Move out of the path of a directional special before contact.

#### Controlled retreat

Move backward from short-range specials when sufficient space exists.

#### Startup interruption

Attempt a legitimate attack during the hero's vulnerable special startup.

### Initial response-attempt probability

15–25% against eligible telegraphed specials.

### Requirements

- No automatic special immunity.
- No universal special counter.
- No blocking unblockable attacks.
- No evasion after an attack has already connected.
- No instant teleportation.
- No perfect input prediction.

### Important

A special that successfully connects must resolve normally.

# PHASE 20 — DEFENSIVE COMMITMENT
## CRITICAL FAIRNESS SYSTEM

Once Saito commits to a defensive response, he cannot instantly switch to another response because the player changes tactics.

### Examples

If Saito commits to Intercepting Kick:

- He must complete the kick.
- If the player avoids it, he misses.
- He enters recovery.
- The player may punish.

If Saito commits to Sidestep:

- He cannot instantly reverse direction.
- He must complete the movement.
- He cannot automatically guard the next attack during the same action.

If Saito commits to Counter:

- He must attempt the counter.
- If no valid attack arrives, he fails.
- He enters punishable recovery.

### Important

No perfect defensive adaptation after commitment.

No instant defense chaining.
