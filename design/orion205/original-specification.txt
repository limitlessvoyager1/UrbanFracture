# URBAN FRACTURE — ORION COMPLETE PLAYABLE CHARACTER IMPLEMENTATION
## Half-Human Cyborg, Electrical Crowd Control, Ranged Combat, Boosters, and System Repair

### PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Create Orion as the sixth playable hero in Urban Fracture.

Orion is a half-human, half-cyborg who escaped an unfinished forced cybernetic conversion performed by the criminal syndicate.

His specialty is:

- Crowd control.
- Ranged attacks.
- Electrical damage.
- Multi-target knockback.
- Enhanced durability.
- Mechanical strength.
- Short-distance booster mobility.

Orion should be especially effective against groups but less efficient against isolated enemies.

**Do not make Orion the strongest hero in every situation.**

Preserve all existing playable heroes, levels, enemy AI, weather systems, controls, and combat mechanics.

Create a reversible checkpoint before implementation.

---

# PART 1 — CHARACTER BACKSTORY

Orion was once an ordinary office worker.

The syndicate kidnapped him and subjected him to an experimental program intended to create controllable cyborg soldiers.

The procedure involved:

- Cybernetic skeletal reinforcement.
- Robotic limb replacement.
- Neural combat programming.
- Enhanced physical strength.
- Enhanced durability.
- Electrical weapon systems.
- Booster-assisted movement.

Orion escaped before the procedure was completed.

As a result, he remains visibly half-human and half-machine.

He retains his personality, memories, and free will.

He is determined to take revenge on the syndicate responsible for his transformation.

### Personality

Orion tries to maintain his sense of humor.

He occasionally makes sarcastic remarks.

However, his physical condition and memories of the experiment can trigger visible anger.

His dialogue and idle behavior should reflect this contrast.

## Running — Heel Boosters

Preserve the game's existing double-tap-forward-and-hold run input.

Orion does not perform an ordinary running animation.

Instead:

1. He leans forward.
2. His boot boosters activate.
3. Small golden-yellow exhaust effects appear.
4. His feet remain near the ground.
5. He accelerates forward.
6. He maintains a controlled boosted movement.
7. Releasing Forward deactivates the boosters.
8. He decelerates and returns to walking or idle.

### Important

The booster run must not resemble uncontrolled sliding.

Use appropriate body lean, leg positioning, acceleration, and deceleration.

Do not let him float above the combat plane.

# PART 5 — STANDARD FOUR-HIT COMBO

## Three Kicks → Spinning Piston Punch

### Sequence

1. Fast front kick.
2. Second kick.
3. Stronger turning kick.
4. Spinning piston punch with robotic right arm.

### Final attack

The robotic fist extends explosively.

The enemy is launched backward.

If the enemy collides with another opponent, apply light collision damage.

### Balance

Moderate total damage.

The final punch provides the main knockback.

### Important

Keep the opponent within range through the first three kicks.

Do not allow the final piston punch to miss because earlier hits push the opponent too far away.

# PART 7 — CHARGED ATTACK
## SYSTEM REPAIR

### Input

Hold Attack for three seconds, then release.

### Function

Restore:

- **17% of Orion's maximum HP.**
- **100% of his defense meter.**

### Health calculation

130 × 0.17 = 22.1 HP.

Use the game's established HP rounding rules.

Do not exceed 130 HP.

### Usage limit

**Once per life.**

The ability must not become available again until Orion loses a life and respawns.

Do not reset usage when entering another room or level.

### Charging restrictions

- Orion cannot move while charging.
- Orion remains vulnerable to attacks.
- Enemy attacks may interrupt charging.
- No invulnerability during charging.
- No Super Armor during charging.

### Animation

1. Orion plants his feet.
2. He lowers his head slightly.
3. Electricity begins flowing through his body.
4. The electrical effect intensifies during charging.
5. At three seconds, a full-charge cue activates.
6. On release, electricity surges through his cybernetic systems.
7. His HP increases.
8. His defense meter refills.
9. He returns to stance.

### Important

Do not restore HP or defense meter before successful activation.

Do not consume the once-per-life use if charging is interrupted before activation.

# PART 14 — FORWARD SPECIAL
## GOLDEN VISOR LASER

### Sequence

1. Orion turns toward the selected direction.
2. His yellow visor begins glowing.
3. He braces his stance.
4. A golden-yellow laser fires from the visor.
5. The beam travels to the visible screen edge.
6. Eligible enemies intersecting the beam receive damage and a brief stun.
7. The beam disappears.
8. Orion enters recovery.

### Balance

This is a powerful ranged crowd-control move.

However:

- It has a clearly readable charge-up.
- Orion cannot move while firing.
- It has substantial recovery.
- Damage is lower against a single target than his strongest focused attacks.
- Bosses have reduced stun duration.
- The beam does not pass through solid level geometry unless explicitly permitted.

### Important

Do not make the beam continuously damage enemies every frame.

Use controlled damage events.

# PART 20 — NORMAL DIFFICULTY BALANCING

Orion's greatest strength is fighting groups.

### Against multiple enemies

He should excel at:

- Creating distance.
- Interrupting coordinated attacks.
- Knocking enemies apart.
- Controlling space.
- Damaging several enemies at once.

### Against one enemy

His crowd-control attacks should be less efficient.

For example:

- Ground Current loses value when only one enemy is present.
- Electric Meteor has a long commitment for one target.
- Golden Visor Laser has high startup and recovery relative to single-target damage.
- Shock Blast's chain damage provides no benefit without nearby enemies.

### Important

Do not artificially reduce every attack's damage against a single enemy.

Balance primarily through opportunity cost, area coverage, and recovery.
