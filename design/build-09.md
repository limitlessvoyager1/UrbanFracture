# Build 09 — reliable catch input and rechargeable defense

B now arms a 0.45-second catch window (previously 0.18) which persists after key release. It can be buffered near the end of an action without cancelling that action. Holding B continues normal frontal blocking. A timed strike catch bypasses temporary post-grab immunity while retaining lane, facing, weapon, airborne, held/downed and action checks. Catch checks use the resolving attack rather than relying on the enemy's live action object. Unarmed one-two/hook and kick/frontKick/roundhouse actions are eligible; current stage AI uses punches and hooks. Existing throw/kick/combo follow-ups are unchanged.

Reina starts with 100 defense. A successful catch consumes 24 once, covering its follow-up. Block costs ceil(raw damage * 1.25), plus 8 for knockdown attacks and 4 for weapons. Affordable blocks retain 75% damage reduction rounded upward. Insufficient defense empties the meter, breaks guard for 0.75 seconds and allows the normal attack damage/knockdown. Regeneration starts one second after spending, at 12 per second, while not blocking or holding. Meter caps at 100 and resets with the level.

Added a shorter mint defense meter below Reina's blue health bar. It turns amber below catch cost, displays CATCH READY during the input window and BROKEN after guard break. Sword readiness stays alongside it. B's guard pose remains visible through a tapped catch window. Guide and accessible status include defense information.

Validation: new tests cover tap/release at +0.4 seconds, grab immunity, punch/hook/kick eligibility, timing expiry, costs by strength/weapon, low-meter block versus catch, guard break, recharge delay/cap, held-guard regeneration restriction and buffered B. Existing combat, renderer, sprite, HUD and full-level tests pass. Browser QA used the actual B keydown/up path against a punch resolving 0.4 seconds later; Reina caught it without damage and the meter dropped to 76. Verified meter placement and readability.
