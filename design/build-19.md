# Build 19 — aligned streets, straight blitz, Carmen and encounter waves

All outdoor facade panels now terminate above a shared sidewalk/curb/asphalt surface rather than scaling their pavement independently. This removes steps at district seams. Outside walk depth is 438–512, keeping feet below background mailboxes and other fixed facade objects; spawn positions and movement respect the same range. Nightlife uses a cropped closed club/lounge frontage and the Velvet Room's exterior doors; the open stocked-bar portion is omitted outdoors. The real bar remains exclusively in the separate venue interior.

Reina's forward-forward-Attack blitz flies immediately: no grounded run-up or spinning kick. New takeoff/straight flying kick/landing art, three trailing dark silhouettes and blue foot energy. It deals 32 damage, launches a victim 260 units with collateral knockdowns, adds a blue impact burst and stronger camera shake, and uses layered bass/crack sound cues when sound is enabled. The .64-second action starts a 1.5-second blitz recovery timer after landing; interrupted attempts also retain a timer. Other ordinary moves remain available during this blitz-specific recovery. HUD displays active/ready/recovery state.

Anti-air attacks have a one-second cooldown after their animation. Recovery from knockdown has an additional .4-second state during which no anti-air can start, supplementing the existing down/get-up exclusion. Marcus can now be picked up from the floor after the falling portion or during get-up; down state, invulnerability and grab immunity clear when the hold succeeds.

Added Carmen, an adult female dirty cop with brownish-blonde ponytail, navy uniform, tactical pants/boots, baton and hand taser. 48 HP (lower than Marcus). Medium-range baton combo deals 8/10/13 with a knockdown finish; close taser deals 18 and stuns an unguarded Reina for one second. At longer range she sometimes leaps forward with a 20-damage kick. If Reina is close during Carmen's get-up, Carmen uses a 16-damage wake-up sweep. Added twelve illustrated poses, walking, fall/ground/get-up states, taser electricity, HUD portrait/name and roster/Sprite Room entry.

Expanded the level from 18 to 41 enemies, deployed in progress-gated groups (5/6/5/6/6/6/7 per district). Additional enemies enter as Reina moves forward rather than all existing on screen from the outset. Retained left-side arrivals. Pending reinforcements prevent premature clear/scene transition; when no enemy is active the HUD instructs Reina to advance. A queue enforces at most one living Frankie and one living Bruno, a stronger guarantee than the onscreen limit.

Jackal is taller (render scale 1.04→1.20) and has 450 HP. He starts with a pipe, drops it and equips a metal bat at 300 HP, then drops the bat and equips a knife at 150 HP. Dropped weapons remain visible briefly. Main weapon damage increases by 2 (pipe24/bat22/knife21), with corresponding slight increases to anti-air and ground follow-up. Attacks have a protected active portion and remain vulnerable during late recovery and longer cooldown. In the alley Jackal and three Marcus enemies appear first; once all Marcus are defeated, two Brunos arrive sequentially (one at a time). Carmen follows after both Brunos are defeated. Victory requires all reinforcements and Jackal to be defeated, even if Jackal falls early.
