# Build 13 — Frankie close combat, throw input, controller access and sword spin

Frankie's chain lash range increases 185→245 with damage 14→16; sweep range 195→260 and damage 16→18. He maintains roughly 150–220 units of separation. At close range he alternates a chain-wrapped fist (20 damage, knockdown) and a front kick (18 damage, knockdown) that drives Reina to roughly 210 units away, constrained by arena edges. The kick is catchable as a leg attack; chain-wrapped punches remain weapon attacks.

Fixed missing held Attack processing during grabs. Holding a direction and Attack can now transition a knee into a throw without releasing/repressing Attack. A directional Attack tapped during a knee is buffered for .5 seconds; input during grab-entry is buffered for .4 seconds. Release/interruption clears pending hold input. Front outer reap/hip and rear German inputs retain existing damage. Browser keyboard hip throw completed and reduced Marcus to 34 HP; simulated raw DualSense + direction + Square also completed a throw. Added actual game.js integration coverage for holding Square through a knee into the hip throw.

Native input now accepts standard gamepads plus a known Sony raw layout (raw Square 0/Cross 1/Circle 2/Triangle 3, L1 4/R1 5/R2 7/Options 9, D-pad hat axis 9 or raw directional buttons). A layout selector supports Auto, Standard/DSX Xbox and DualSense raw, saved locally. Active devices are selected by input rather than permanently preferring an idle first pad. A live controller panel shows device, pressed button indices and stick axes. API/embedding access failures are reported with an Open Game in Own Tab link; touchpad mouse movement is explicitly distinguished from gamepad input. Focus, neutral-input and disconnect protections remain. No physical controller was available for testing; hardware/driver visibility still needs user verification through the live panel.

Reina's neutral sword special is now Crescent Spin: six distinct pivoting/front/back/side sword poses blended through a complete turn. It hits each nearby enemy once during its active spin, still costs 12 HP and deals 26 damage; invulnerability lasts through the .65-second move. Active throws/specials no longer blink transparent due to invincibility flashing.

Built-in generated asset: dist/assets/sprites/spin-combat-v1.png, metadata dist/spin-combat-data.js. Prompt used the existing Reina/Frankie references to create a transparent 3x3 pixel-art atlas: six consecutive standing 360-degree sword-spin poses with pivoting feet/hair/sash, followed by Frankie chain-wrapped fist windup, extended power punch and front kick; preserve identities/costumes, isolated full bodies, no labels/effects/opponents.

Validation: all existing regressions and the full six-encounter route pass. New tests cover knee/entry/held throw inputs, raw Sony button/hat mapping and blocked controller access, longer chain damage/range, fist knockdown, kick displacement, unique six-pose spin rendering and once-per-target radial damage. Browser verified keyboard throw release/completion, simulated raw Sony detection/throw, sword-spin art and chain-wrapped fist art. Temporary QA fixture removed before publication.
