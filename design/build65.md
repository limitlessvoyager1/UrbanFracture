# Build 65: hotel and kitchen

The lobby marker sits near the sidewalk with a contrasting backing and points toward the entrance. The lobby background was edited with the built-in image generation tool to add individually varied waiting guests, hotel seating, luggage and service doors. A separate generated side-scrolling commercial kitchen shows a cooler, sinks, food storage, prep areas, ovens, fryers and the rear exit. Both assets are in dist/assets. The lobby and kitchen each have their own encounter and transition.
