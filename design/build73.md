# Build 73: Extended nightlife

The hotel route adds 1100 world units of nightlife between the hotel service alley and the existing nightclub. Three staggered enemies use that space; Brick and his escorts wait at the original nightclub until the other waves are defeated. The regular route is unchanged.

The built-in image generation tool created `dist/assets/hotel-nightlife-extra73.png` from the existing nightlife artwork as a style reference.
