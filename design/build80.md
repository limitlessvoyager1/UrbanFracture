# Skylar Nova palette update

The built-in image generation tool edited the previous portrait and pose sheets to give Skylar violet hair, blue eyes, a black star-emblem wrestling top, violet-to-black tights with stars on black, black boots with violet laces, and violet gloves. A further targeted edit placed the stars against black sections and added the top emblem to the acrobatic poses while preserving pose positions and sheet dimensions.

Assets: `dist/assets/nova-portrait80.png`, and `dist/assets/sprites/nova-motion80.png`, `nova-attacks80.png`, `nova-grapple80.png`, and `nova-acrobat80.png`.
