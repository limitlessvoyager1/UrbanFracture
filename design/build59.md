# Build 59 sprite notes

Jackal's connected walking atlas `dist/assets/sprites/jackal-walk59.png` and held-pose atlas `dist/assets/sprites/jackal-held59.png` were generated with `boss-v1.png` as the character reference. Prompts requested the same short dark hair, burgundy shirt, black vest, dark trousers and boots: four full-body grounded walking poses, and two separate restrained front/rear poses, in the existing 16-bit illustrated style on transparent backgrounds.

Kevin's front/rear held atlas `dist/assets/sprites/kevin-held59.png` was generated from `kevin-brick-v1.png`, preserving his hunter-green tank top, tattooed arms and boots. Reina's rear-grab sprite `dist/assets/sprites/reina-rear-grab59.png` was generated from `reina-v1.png` with one arm reaching to the opponent's shoulder and one at waist height, preserving her outfit, gloves, sandals and sheathed sword.
